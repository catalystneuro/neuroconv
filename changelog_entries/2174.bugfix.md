`configure_backend` no longer raises `NotImplementedError` on an extension container that is not a `TimeSeries` but has a `data` or `timestamps` dataset, such as an ndx-events 0.2 `Events` in a file streamed from DANDI and read against its cached namespaces. Those datasets are now chunked and compressed like `TimeSeries` data, including the ndx-events 0.2 `LabeledEvents` and `TTLs` that were previously skipped.
