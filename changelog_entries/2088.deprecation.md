Deprecations dated "on or after February 2027" now name v0.12.0, and those dated "on or after August 2027" name v0.13.0, so every pending deprecation names the version it will be removed in. They cover `write_as` and `es_key` on the ecephys interfaces and writers, the nested video camera device, the deprecated Bruker TIFF interfaces, the TDT fiber photometry multi-series path, `include_roi_acceptance`, `make_or_load_nwbfile`, the list-based metadata format, neo-style NIDQ channel ids, `digital_channel_groups` and the LightningPose per-series conversion options.
