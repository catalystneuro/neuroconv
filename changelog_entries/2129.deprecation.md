Series written from a recording with several segments are now named with an unpadded `Segment{index}` suffix, for example `ElectricalSeriesSegment0` instead of `ElectricalSeries0`, and each `ElectricalSeries` description ends with "Segment {index} of {count}.". The default series name of `add_recording_to_nwbfile` for `acquisition` and `processing/FilteredEphys` is now `ElectricalSeries` instead of `ElectricalSeriesRaw` and `ElectricalSeriesProcessed`, and `AxonRecordingInterface` and `PlexonLFPInterface` now write `ElectricalSeries` and `ElectricalSeriesLFP` instead of `ElectricalSeriesRaw` and `ElectricalSeriesLF`.
