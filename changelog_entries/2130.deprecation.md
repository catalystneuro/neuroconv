`get_timestamps`, `set_aligned_timestamps`, `set_aligned_segment_timestamps`, `set_aligned_starting_time`, `set_aligned_segment_starting_times` and `align_by_interpolation` on the recording interfaces are deprecated and will be removed in v0.13.0. Use `interface.temporal_alignment` instead. `align_by_interpolation` used to raise `NotImplementedError` on a recording with several segments and now remaps every segment.
