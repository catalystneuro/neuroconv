The default chunk shape of the `waveform_mean` and `waveform_sd` columns of the `Units` table now holds whole templates, as many as fit in 10 MB, so reading the template of one unit reads a single chunk. The generic estimator split the samples and electrodes axes as well as the units axis: on a `(898, 128, 40)` float32 array the chunk goes from `(726, 99, 33)`, where one template is spread over 4 of 8 chunks, to `(488, 128, 40)`.
