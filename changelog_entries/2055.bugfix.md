`add_sorting_analyzer_to_nwbfile` now writes the waveform sampling rate, unit and `time_before_peak_in_ms` alignment point that the analyzer already knows, where it previously wrote the templates alone and left all three at their defaults. The unit is `microvolts` for an analyzer whose traces are scaled, which is the unit spikeinterface returns them in, and `a.u.` for one built on a recording without gains.
