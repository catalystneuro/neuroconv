Added `OptiTrackInterface` for writing OptiTrack Motive CSV exports to NWB. Each rigid body is written as a `SpatialSeries` for its position, a `TimeSeries` for its orientation and, when the export has it, a `TimeSeries` for its mean marker error. The labeled bones and markers of each skeleton or marker set are written as one `ndx-pose` `MultiCameraPoseEstimation` container, with the orientation of each bone as a `TimeSeries`.
