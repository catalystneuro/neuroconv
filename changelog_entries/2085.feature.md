Added `OptiTrackInterface` for writing the positions and rotations in OptiTrack Motive CSV exports to NWB. Each asset type in the export (rigid bodies, rigid body marker constraints and labeled markers) is written as one `ndx-pose` `PoseEstimation` container with one keypoint per asset, and the rotation of each rigid body is written to the behavior module as a `SpatialSeries` in degrees for Euler angle exports or as a `TimeSeries` with columns w, x, y, z for quaternion exports.
