`IntanDigitalInterface`, `IntanAnalogEventsInterface` and `IntanConverter` take `event_extraction_configuration`, where a digital source is a port (`digital_inputs`, `digital_outputs`) whose lines are addressed by their position counted from 0 and an ADC source is its channel; `IntanConverter` routes each entry to the interface that reads its `source` and no longer takes the unreleased `detection_configuration`.
