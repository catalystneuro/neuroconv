Added general-anatomy annotation to `neuroconv.tools.ontology`: `infer_anatomy_ontology_metadata` resolves `ndx-pose` `Skeleton.nodes` entries (pose-estimation keypoints, e.g. `"Snout"`, `"forepaw"`) to a UBERON term and writes it to `metadata["PoseEstimation"]["ontology"]["anatomy"]`, and `add_anatomy_external_resources` writes those stated terms into the file as HERD references. A conversion runs it automatically, so it does nothing unless the metadata carries the map, and the lookup is species-agnostic and independent of the brain-region atlases.
