Fiber photometry now requires ndx-fiber-photometry 0.3 and ndx-ophys-devices 0.4: `fiber_insertion` and a virus injection's new `viral_injection_coordinates` take `StereotacticPosition` fields (`anatomical_target`, `origin`, `orientation`, `x_in_mm`, `y_in_mm`, `z_in_mm`) in place of the AP/ML/DV, depth, `location`, `hemisphere` and `reference` fields, and `gain` and `gain_unit` move from the photodetector model to the `Photodetector`.
