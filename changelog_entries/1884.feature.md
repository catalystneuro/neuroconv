Added strain annotation to `neuroconv.tools.ontology`: `infer_strain_ontology_metadata` resolves `Subject.strain` (a canonical designation, an informal spelling such as `"black 6"`, or a likely typo) to an RRID term for eight common laboratory rodent strains and writes it to `metadata["Subject"]["ontology"]["strain"]`, and `add_strain_external_resource` writes that stated term into the file as a HERD reference. A conversion runs it automatically, so it does nothing unless the metadata carries the term, and `validate_strain` warns without blocking on informal spellings and typos.
