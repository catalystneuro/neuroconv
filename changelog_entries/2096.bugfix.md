`add_segmentation_to_nwbfile`, `write_segmentation_to_nwbfile` and the `add_to_nwbfile` of the segmentation interfaces no longer accept `mask_type`, `include_roi_centroids`, `include_background_segmentation` and `background_plane_segmentation_name`, which were silently ignored: masks are written in the format the extractor provides, ROI centroids are not written, and background segmentation is not supported. Passing them now raises a `TypeError` instead of writing a file without what was asked for.
