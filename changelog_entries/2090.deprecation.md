Arguments after the path in an interface's `__init__`, after `nwbfile` and `metadata` in `add_to_nwbfile`, and after the extractor, `nwbfile` and `metadata` in `add_imaging_to_nwbfile` and `add_segmentation_to_nwbfile` are now keyword-only, and passing them positionally raises `TypeError`. Their deprecation was due between June and September 2026.
