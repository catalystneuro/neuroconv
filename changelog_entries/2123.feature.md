`InternalVideoInterface` now supports `interface.temporal_alignment`, with one time-bearing object keyed by its `metadata_key`, so the video can be shifted, placed with `move_start_to`, given replacement timestamps or remapped. A video that was only shifted or placed is still written with a starting time and the header frame rate, and an unaligned video now starts at the time its container stores for the first frame, usually zero, as `ExternalVideoInterface` does.
