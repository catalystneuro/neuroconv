The sorting interfaces take a `metadata_key`, defaulting to a constant per format such as `kilosort_sorting`, and `add_sorting_to_nwbfile` and the sorting analyzer writers take one too. The sorting's entry in `metadata["Ecephys"]["Sortings"]` names the table it writes into through `units_table_metadata_key`, and the table is declared in `metadata["Ecephys"]["UnitsTables"]` with its `name` and `description`, so several sortings can write into one table.
