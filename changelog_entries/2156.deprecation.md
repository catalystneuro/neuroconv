`units_name` and `units_description` on `BaseSortingExtractorInterface.add_to_nwbfile` and `PhySortingInterface.add_to_nwbfile` are deprecated and will be removed in v0.13.0. Set the table's `name` and `description` in `metadata["Ecephys"]["UnitsTables"]` instead. `PhySortingInterface` no longer describes its units table as "Imported from Phy".
